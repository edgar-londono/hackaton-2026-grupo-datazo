# Ideas after the MVP

These ideas are outside the MVP. Consider them only after the listing cleanup and property cards are complete.

## Verify supplied geographic records

- Compare existing coordinates and labels with official public records.
- Change a value only when the source identifies the same place without doubt.
- Keep the original value, corrected value, evidence, source, license, and check date.
- Leave conflicting or ambiguous records unchanged and explain the limit.

## Add more public points of interest

Possible source areas include:

- INDER sports facilities, such as courts and sports centers: [Medata INDER catalog](https://medata.gov.co/node/16735).
- Schools and education institutions: [Ministry of Education school search system](https://www.mineducacion.gov.co/sistemasinfo/sistemapbm/Sistemas-de-Informacion-Especializados/259652%3ASistema-de-Informacion-Buscando-Colegio).
- Museums and cultural spaces: [SINIC arts and cultural spaces dataset](https://www.datos.gov.co/Cultura/Mapa-de-Espacios-de-las-Artes-las-Culturas-y-los-S/p9n3-av9n).

Before adding a source, record its owner, direct download or service URL, update date, license, fields, and verification method. Do not add a record when its identity or location cannot be checked. These sources are suggestions, not approved data imports.
